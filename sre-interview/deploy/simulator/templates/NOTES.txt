The simulator is posting webhooks at {{ .Values.ingest.url }}, one every
{{ div .Values.traffic.meanIntervalMs 1000 }}s or so, in bursts, with quiet gaps.

Watch it:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs -l app.kubernetes.io/name=simulator -f

It logs the counts every {{ div .Values.summaryMs 1000 }}s. For a line per record:
  helm -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} upgrade {{ .Release.Name }} ./simulator --reuse-values --set otel.logLevel=debug

To stop the traffic without uninstalling anything:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} scale deploy/{{ include "simulator.fullname" . }} --replicas=0
